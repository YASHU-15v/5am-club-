About The Club

Welcome to the Wayanad 5 AM Club, an exclusive sanctuary nestled in the misty hills of Kerala. We believe the magic happens in the early hours.

For Parents: Find the quiet peace you deserve. Reconnect with yourself while enjoying the tranquil environment, away from the hustle of daily life.

For Couples: Experience profound romance with sunrise walks, private wellness sessions, and luxurious, secluded accommodations tailored for intimate getaways.

For Seniors: Embrace holistic rejuvenation. Our gentle yoga programs, mindful meditation sessions, and pristine, crisp morning air are perfect for health and longevity.

The 5 AM Experience

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What activities are included in the 5 AM schedule?

Our morning routine includes guided meditation, breathwork, gentle yoga facing the sunrise, and a customized nature walk through the tea estates.

2. Is the environment suitable for senior citizens?

Absolutely. We have specifically designed accessible pathways, and our wellness experts tailor activities to ensure they are safe, gentle, and highly beneficial for seniors.

3. Can couples book private wellness sessions?

Yes, couples can reserve private dawn-yoga sessions and secluded breakfast experiences to ensure a completely uninterrupted romantic retreat.

4. Do you provide healthy dietary options?

Yes, our luxury kitchen prepares farm-to-table, organic meals crafted to support holistic health, with custom menus available for specific dietary requirements.

5. How do I secure a reservation?

Simply click the "Book / Login" button at the top of the page, fill out your desired date and phone number, and our concierge will contact you to finalize details.
